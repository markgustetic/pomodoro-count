# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project about counting tomatoes.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Someone filing a confusing bug report is
  trying to help.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. "This doesn't fit what the app is
  for" is a legitimate and final answer.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, or
publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report problems to mark.gustetic@gmail.com. Reports stay confidential. Maintainers
may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this document, and may block
accounts for repeated or serious violations.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
trimmed to fit a project of this size.
